FAQ

Questions people ask

What is DEBONE Digital Hub?

DEBONE Digital Hub is a Nigerian online platform for NIN and BVN verification, slip downloads, IPE clearance, NIN validation, personalisation, and CAC, TIN, ID and vehicle checks. It is part of the DEBONE brand, serving clients since 1997.

How do I verify a NIN online?

Create an account, fund your wallet with Paystack, choose NIN Verification, enter the 11-digit NIN and confirm. The result appears on screen and is saved in My Results.

Do I pay again if I close the page?

No. Every successful result is saved for 30 days. Open it again and download the slip as often as you like at no cost.

What is the difference between agent and individual prices?

Every service is open to everyone. Verified agents pay a lower agent price, individuals pay a higher individual price. Apply under Agent Verification to get agent prices.

How do I fund my wallet?

All wallet funding runs through Paystack. We never see or store your card details.

Is my data protected?

We handle personal data in line with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, keep results for a limited time, and offer two-step login.

What happens if a check fails?

The fee returns to your wallet automatically when a check or agency request cannot be completed.
