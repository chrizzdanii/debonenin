FAQ

IPE Clearance questions

What is IPE clearance?

IPE stands for Instant Processing Error. Clearance is the request that asks for a stuck NIN record to be processed again.

How long does IPE clearance take?

Time varies by case. Request status is shown in your dashboard, and checking status costs nothing.

What if my IPE request fails?

The fee returns to your wallet automatically.

Can agents get lower prices?

Yes. Verified agents pay the agent price, which is lower than the individual price.
