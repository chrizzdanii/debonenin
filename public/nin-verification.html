FAQ

NIN Verification questions

How do I verify a NIN online?

Create a free account, fund your wallet with Paystack, open NIN Verification, type the 11-digit NIN and confirm. The result appears on screen and is saved to My Results.

Can I verify a NIN with a phone number?

Yes. Use the NIN by phone option. Enter the phone number linked to the NIN and the profile is returned if a record exists.

How much does a NIN verification cost?

The exact price is shown before you confirm each check. Verified agents pay lower prices than individuals, and you are never charged for status checks.

Is my result saved if I close the page?

Yes. Every successful result stays in My Results for 30 days, and you can view or download it again at no extra cost.
